# Privacy policy draft — Kern

Last updated: 2026-07-11

Kern is a markdown reader and writing workspace for Android. It is designed around local files and a quiet document-first workflow.

This draft should be reviewed before public launch and updated to match the exact production build submitted to Google Play.

## Information you create in Kern

Kern is designed to work with markdown documents and folders you choose on your Android device. Your markdown files and document content are not uploaded to Kern-operated servers.

## Local file access

Kern uses Android's file access flows so you can choose files or folders to work with. Access is limited to the files or folders you select through Android system interfaces.

## Analytics and diagnostics

Kern uses Firebase Analytics for launch analytics. Google/Firebase may collect app interaction, device, and diagnostic information such as app events, device identifiers, install/referrer data, and technical information used for analytics.

Kern does not use this analytics path to upload the contents of your markdown documents.

## Accounts

Kern does not currently require a Kern account.

## Sharing

Kern does not sell personal information. If analytics remains enabled, analytics data may be processed by Google/Firebase according to their terms and privacy policies.
