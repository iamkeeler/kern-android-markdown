Privacy

Privacy Policy

Kern is built as a local-first markdown reader and writing workspace for Android. This page outlines our commitment to data minimization and document privacy.

Local documents

Kern operates directly on the markdown files and project folders you select on your Android device using system file pickers. Document contents remain strictly on your device and are not uploaded to external servers.

Diagnostics & telemetry

Kern uses minimal diagnostic counters and error reports to maintain application stability across Android OS versions. No document contents, titles, or body text are included in diagnostic payloads.

Website privacy

This static website does not require account creation, track users across third-party networks, or store persistent tracking cookies.
